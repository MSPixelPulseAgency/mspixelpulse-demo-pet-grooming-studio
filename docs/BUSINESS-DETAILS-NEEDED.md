Please supply:

- Confirmed trading/legal name, operator and permission to represent the business.
- Studio address or verified mobile service area, public phone/email, opening hours and access information.
- Approved service menu, species/breed/age limits, staff qualifications if claimed, actual appointment process and durations.
- Approved prices, currency/tax treatment, add-on rates and pricing conditions.
- Vaccination, health, handling, cancellation, late-arrival, matting and pickup policies.
- Real booking provider/calendar or approved booking backend, availability rules and confirmation workflow.
- Approved privacy/contact retention wording, responsible business identity and consent language.
- Genuine reviews with publication permission, and business-owned/released staff, premises and matching transformation photos.
